The RUVA flow

Every job moves the same four steps.

Each homeowner card shows where the job stands, so reps and the office never have to text each other to find out.

Knock the door, pin the house, log the homeowner and the storm that hit it.

For the sales rep

Built for the work between the truck and the front door.

Every house starts with a pin. The homeowner, roof, claim, photos, follow-up and commission all live on that pin, so nothing gets lost between the first knock and the final check.

Satellite canvassing

Current, high-resolution satellite imagery. Pin the house you're standing at with one tap, or press and hold on any roof. Each status has its own icon, so a whole street reads at a glance.

Leads & follow-ups

Every door becomes a lead. Schedule a follow-up in one tap and get a reminder when it's due.

Inspection photos

Organize photos by slope and mark hail and wind damage directly on the image. Photos stay with the job — not buried in your camera roll.

Inspection reports

Generate a polished PDF report with photos and storm details, ready for the homeowner or the adjuster.

Storm tracking

National Weather Service hail and wind reports, plotted on the map with sizes, dates and active alerts.

Route planning

Build an optimized route to the houses you choose and send it to Apple Maps or Google Maps in one tap.

For the owner and the office

Run every job from one dashboard.

The office side is where small companies lose money: missed supplements, wrong shingle colors, crews paid by memory and no idea what a job really made. RUVA puts all of it on one screen.

Supplements that don't slip

Paste in the adjuster's scope and RUVA checks it against the measurements for what's missing: drip edge, starter, ridge cap, ice and water, steep charge, O&P. One click turns them into supplements ready to send.

  • Approved, partial and denied, tracked per item
  • Approved money flows into the job total and invoice
  • A reminder when the adjuster goes quiet

Material orders from the signed contract

Set up your brands, products and colors once. When the homeowner picks their shingle, the order fills itself: shingles, starter and ridge cap in the right brand and color, quantities from the measurements.

  • Good, Better, Best proposals the homeowner picks from
  • The order won't send until every color is picked
  • Change orders signed and added to the job

Crews scheduled, texted and paid

Each job gets a labor order from the crew's rate and the measured squares. The crew gets a text with the job sheet: address, shingle and color, notes and their pay. No crew logins, no extra app.

  • Steep, two-story, decking and other extras
  • See what you owe each crew, and mark it paid
  • Warranty registration after the install

FAQ

Common questions

What does RUVA stand for?

Roofing Unified Visual Analytics. It's also the four steps every job moves through: Record, Upload, Verify, Approve.

Do my reps need to download anything?

No. They open RUVA in Safari or Chrome and add it to their home screen. It opens full-screen with its own icon, like any other app, on iPhone and Android.

We already use another CRM. Do we have to switch everything at once?

No. The 60-day pilot runs alongside what you use now. Run a few jobs through RUVA, compare, and decide. Your existing leads can be imported from a spreadsheet.

Do crews need a login?

No. Crews get a text with a link to the job sheet: address, shingle and color, notes and their pay. Homeowners get their own private link too.

Does it connect to QuickBooks and EagleView?

Those connections are in approval with each partner, along with supplier ordering, payments and financing. Everything else works on day one.

Who owns the data?

You do. Export it anytime. Each company's data is kept separate. We never sell your data or show ads. See our privacy policy.

Where does the storm data come from?

Hail and wind reports come from the National Weather Service. They show reported storm activity, not confirmed damage to a specific roof, so an inspection is always recommended.
